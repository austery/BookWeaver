我曾相信，在您的帮助下，我可以创造出一些有持久价值并引以为傲的东西。或许不是伯克希尔哈撒韦公司（Berkshire Hathaway），但会是苏黎世（Zürich）的一个“双胞胎”。正如德国人会说的，“Klein aber fein”（小而美）。此外，我们进展顺利。我拥有合适的团队，管理着4.7亿美元的资产，我们正在形成我一直追求的势头，但正如意第绪语谚语所说，“Der mentsh trakht und gott lakht”（人算不如天算）。或者，用迈克·泰森（Mike Tyson）的话说，“每个人都有计划，直到他们被拳头打在脸上。”

我被打在脸上的那一拳发生在2024年11月下旬。克洛斯特斯（Klosters）的雪来得早，我那天和孩子们滑了一整天雪，但在返回苏黎世的路上，我突发了大发作（grand mal seizure），也就是癫痫发作。

Hirslanden医院的核磁共振（MRI）显示，我的头部有一个小肿瘤，位于一个被认为可以安全切除的位置。四天后，我接受了开颅手术，肿瘤被完全切除。尽管肿瘤很小且外观呈良性，但组织学证实是4级胶质母细胞瘤（glioblastoma），即GBM。

由于这里是瑞士，我很快被转移到GBM的标准治疗方案——即Stupp方案：从2024年12月23日开始，到我的生日2025年2月4日结束的放疗和化疗疗程。你们中的一些人也看到我佩戴了Optune设备，希望它能防止复发。

The tumor was small, the doctors caught it early, and I immediately underwent the appropriate treatment, so my chances of beating the worst outcomes were good, and I continued my life as normal. 肿瘤很小，医生发现得早，我立即接受了适当的治疗，因此我避免最坏结果的可能性很大，我也继续过着正常的生活。然而，这种状况在2025年9月发生了变化，当时一次不清楚的核磁共振（MRI）和轻微（或局灶性）癫痫发作导致了第二次开颅手术，并得出结论：癌症已经复发。

这次复发改变了海蓝宝基金的评估。此前，我曾满怀希望和期待，认为癌症已缓解，并且我们资本增值的首要任务可以保持不变。复发意味着我不能再这样说了。

While I continue to have every hope of living a long and productive life, that is less likely than before. The range of outcomes has widened considerably. Faced with that uncertainty, I knew I had to reprioritize my life.

尽管我目前的心智和投资能力完好无损，但我们无法知道这种状况何时会改变。我的状况可能会长期稳定，也可能不会。虽然我仍然满怀希望能够过上长寿而富有成效的生活，但这比以前的可能性要小。结果的范围已大大扩大。面对这种不确定性，我明白我必须重新调整我生活的优先级。

Up to this point, despite my many outside interests, my top priority was to continue to compound your capital at Aquamarine Fund. This is no longer the case. My top priority has become my health and spending precious time with my family.

直到此刻，尽管我有许多业余爱好，我的首要任务是在海蓝宝基金继续为您的资本增值。现在情况并非如此。我的首要任务已变为我的健康，以及与家人共度宝贵时光。

我最初的想法是给你们这些外部投资者一个选择：那些愿意的可以拿回资本，而另一些则可以继续投资。然而，在征求了家人、团队、董事、法律顾问和挚友的意见后，我得到了明确的信息，那就是现在返还资金并以良好的姿态结束我的投资生涯会好得多。

这并非我的首选，我也不情愿地采取了这一步。这是由医疗状况而非对市场或我们策略缺乏信心所迫的决定。虽然我无法改变我的诊断结果，但我可以控制我如何应对它。唯一光荣的道路是采取透明行动，并在任何可能的衰退发生之前保护您的利益。

Winding down the fund does not sit easily with me but I do it with a heart full of gratitude rather than regret. I have valued each of you---for your trust, your patience, and your belief in me and my team. That trust made everything possible. It was, and is, the foundation.

清算基金让我心有不甘，但我心怀感激而非遗憾地这样做。我珍视你们每一个人——感谢你们的信任、耐心以及对我和我的团队的信念。这份信任使一切成为可能。它曾经是，现在也是基石。

**LIQUIDATING THE PORTFOLIO**

**清算投资组合**

Once we had taken the decision, we knew that we would need to raise \$235 million in order to meet all the cash returns to investors at the end of the year. So I sat down with David and mapped out what I wanted the portfolio to look like as of year-end. We have always kept the portfolio in liquid securities that were easy to price and liquidate if we had to. We have also kept an updated "days to liquidation" analysis for risk-management purposes. I've always wanted us to be able to liquidate the portfolio easily if necessary. In other words, I always kept an eye on the possibility that an unexpected rainy day was just around the corner.

一旦我们做出决定，我们知道将需要筹集2.35亿美元，以满足年底所有投资者的现金返还需求。所以我与大卫（David）坐下来，规划了年底时我希望投资组合呈现的样子。我们始终将投资组合保持在流动性强的证券中，这些证券在必要时易于定价和清算。我们还出于风险管理目的，保留了更新的“清算天数”分析。我一直希望我们能在必要时轻松清算投资组合。换句话说，我总是关注着意想不到的艰难时刻可能就在眼前。

That meant that David could go about an orderly liquidation of approximately half the portfolio in order to deliver the cash to you. We've sold what we needed to sell to meet the year-end redemption requests and to return capital to investors. David executed the trades through Sanford Bernstein and IIFL Capital.

这意味着大卫（David）可以有条不紊地清算大约一半的投资组合，以便向您交付现金。我们已经出售了所需出售的资产，以满足年末赎回请求并向投资者返还资本。大卫通过Sanford Bernstein和IIFL Capital执行了这些交易。

This wasn't a difficult process. With the exception of one security, Seritage Growth Properties, which took a little longer to sell down without impacting the price, we had no liquidity issues. We were able to sell the shares we needed to without the sales becoming a meaningful proportion of daily volume.

这并不是一个困难的过程。除了一个证券，Seritage Growth Properties，在不影响价格的情况下清算花费了更长的时间，我们没有遇到流动性问题。我们能够出售所需的股份，且销售额并未占每日交易量的很大比例。

David also reviewed execution closely. The vast majority of trades were done at VWAP (volume-weighted average price), or within a very tight tolerance of the VWAP. The goal was to make sure we weren't being disadvantaged by brokers during the liquidation.

大卫（David）也密切审查了执行情况。绝大多数交易均以成交量加权平均价（VWAP）完成，或在VWAP的非常小的容差范围内。目标是确保我们在清算过程中不被经纪人利用。

This was the largest liquidation I'm ever likely to do. We paid the highest aggregate brokerage fees we've ever paid in Aquamarine's history---yet they were in the order of less than 10 basis points, which is reasonable.

这可能是我做过的最大规模的清算。我们支付了海蓝宝基金历史上最高的总经纪费用——然而，这些费用不到10个基点，这是合理的。

我还超越了从研究他人举动中获得的洞察力。我意识到品牌教育与债券的信用评级没有太大区别，只是评级对象是人，因此，我曾一度对营利性教育部门投入大量资金，对DeVry、Apollo Education和Interboro College（通过EVCI职业学院）进行了大量投资。我还走向国际，投资了新加坡的Raffles Education Corporation、菲律宾的Far Eastern Education Corporation、阿曼的Majan University College以及巴西的Anhanguera Educaçional和Estácio Participações。

在理解了垄断性收费公路的强大力量后，我投资了欧洲隧道债券，这又促成了对其他金融基础设施和支付系统的投资：Investment Technology Group、Knight Trading、西联汇款和速汇金、房地美、万事达卡、印度能源交易所和美国运通。

There were plenty of mistakes of various kinds along the way. Sometimes I sold out way too soon: CRISIL, for example, in which I turned what could have been a potential 200X+ return into a mere 7-bagger. I also did not manage to hold on to Aflac, Discover Financial Services, Moutai, Liberty Media, and McDonald's, despite their being fine compounding machines.

一路上犯了各种各样的错误。有时我卖出得太早：例如CRISIL，我将原本可能获得200倍以上回报的投资变成了仅仅7倍。尽管Aflac、Discover Financial Services、茅台、Liberty Media和麦当劳都是优秀的复合增长机器，但我未能一直持有它们。

At times, I was spooked out of the name at the wrong time. One example is Moody's in the aftermath of the 2008 financial crisis, for fear of litigation. In the case of LabCorp of America, I missed out on 200X for fear of insolvency that never materialized.

有时，我在错误的时机因为恐惧而退出。一个例子是2008年金融危机后对穆迪的恐惧，担心诉讼。在LabCorp of America的案例中，我因为担心从未发生的破产而错失了200倍的收益。

More than once I was lucky to have made the money that I did. It was only in retrospect that I realized I'd been running through a bomb factory with a lit match. Although I made a lot of money in the for-profit education space---Raffles Education, DeVry, and EVCI Career Colleges were multi-bagger exits for me---I was constantly running the risk of adverse regulations from the government. It was by dint of pure luck that I sold out of my US for-profit colleges before the election of President Obama in 2008, which all but destroyed the sector. Similarly, I sold out of Freddie Mac not because I was smart, but simply because I questioned. If Warren and Charlie thought that their underwriting was no longer risk-averse and at the same time smart people like Michael Burry and Bill Ackman were betting against the housing market, I had no business being long.

不止一次，我很幸运赚到了钱。事后我才意识到，我当时带着点燃的火柴在炸弹工厂里穿行。尽管我在营利性教育领域赚了很多钱——Raffles Education、DeVry和EVCI Career Colleges为我带来了多倍收益的退出——但我一直面临政府不利法规的风险。我纯粹是靠运气，在2008年奥巴马总统当选之前卖掉了我的美国营利性大学，那次选举几乎摧毁了这个行业。同样，我卖掉房地美并非因为我聪明，而仅仅是因为我产生了疑问。如果沃伦和查理认为他们的承保不再规避风险，同时迈克尔·伯里和比尔·阿克曼这样的聪明人都在做空房地产市场，那么我也没有理由做多。

There were positions that were simply "dead money." The game was set up in such a way that although I was unlikely to lose a lot, I was unlikely to win. That was the case with the Brazilian, Omani, and Filipino education companies, and with Eurotunnel, which represented too juicy a financial opportunity for the banks not to take advantage of the bondholders.

有些头寸简直就是“死钱”。游戏的设计方式使得我不太可能损失很多，但也不太可能赢。巴西、阿曼和菲律宾的教育公司以及欧洲隧道的情况就是如此，欧洲隧道对银行来说是一个太诱人的金融机会，以至于它们不会利用债券持有人。

One area in which I made decent money but wish that I'd never entered is resource extraction--- mining and related investments. In this area the investments range from Fortescue Metals Group in Australia to London Mining, Cresud in Argentina, Potash Corporation of Saskatchewan, Jordan Phosphates, Teekay Offshore, and a few others.

有一个领域我赚了不少钱，但希望自己从未涉足，那就是资源开采——采矿及相关投资。在这个领域，投资范围从澳大利亚的Fortescue Metals Group到伦敦矿业、阿根廷的Cresud、萨斯喀彻温省钾肥公司、约旦磷酸盐、Teekay Offshore以及其他一些公司。

These businesses are cyclical, with boom-and-bust cycles, but to the investor they always look as if they are about to turn non-cyclical and become endless profitable money machines. This rarely happens. That said, there is money to be made in these businesses but it requires very careful study and insider-like knowledge, and on top of that, impeccable timing. In my case this culminated with my largest portfolio loss---Horsehead Holding---but it was largely neutralized by the big wins in Fortescue, London Mining, and Micron Technology.

这些业务是周期性的，有繁荣和萧条的周期，但对投资者来说，它们总是看起来好像即将变得非周期性，并成为永无止境的盈利机器。这种情况很少发生。尽管如此，这些业务中确实有钱可赚，但这需要非常仔细的研究和类似内部人士的知识，最重要的是，需要完美的时机。在我的案例中，这最终导致了我最大的投资组合损失——Horsehead Holding——但很大程度上被Fortescue、伦敦矿业和美光科技的巨大成功所抵消。

When one looks at the bucket as a whole, one sees a lot of activity for not much gain. The portfolio activity was bad enough but what I really regret is the time spent thinking about the space: What the marginal cost curve is; which mines are, or might be, coming online; how demand might decline if there is a recession; who has the balance sheet strength to see it through and who doesn't. All of this is ephemeral knowledge with a short half-life. Even if there is money to be made, there are better ways to spend one's time. If I were starting my investment career from scratch, I'd leave it to someone who wants to play the resource-extraction game.

当人们整体看待这个领域时，会发现很多活动并没有带来太多收益。投资组合的活跃度已经够糟糕了，但我真正后悔的是花在思考这个领域的时间：边际成本曲线是什么；哪些矿山正在或可能投入运营；如果发生经济衰退，需求可能会如何下降；谁有足够的资产负债表实力度过难关，谁又没有。所有这些都是短暂的知识，半衰期很短。即使有钱可赚，也有更好的方式来打发时间。如果我从头开始我的投资生涯，我会把它留给那些想玩资源开采游戏的人。

Then there are many mistakes of omission---where the company was right there and I had the analysis at hand but I did not act. Over a Cornish pasty on the King's Road, Nick Sleep and Qais Zakaria shared their analysis of Amazon and Costco. I did not bite. Although I understood the economics of credit ratings, payment systems, and financial exchanges, I never invested in Visa, S&P Global, MSCI, or any of the big financial exchanges like the New York Stock Exchange, CBOE, and others.

此外还有许多因疏忽而犯的错误——公司就在那里，我手头有分析，但我却没有采取行动。在国王大道上吃康沃尔馅饼时，尼克·斯利普和凯斯·扎卡里亚分享了他们对亚马逊和好市多的分析。我没有入手。尽管我理解信用评级、支付系统和金融交易所的经济原理，但我从未投资过Visa、标普全球、MSCI，以及任何大型金融交易所，如纽约证券交易所、芝加哥期权交易所等。

There was also a meaningful number of companies in which I bought a few shares, held them for a while, and sold them---not making a lot of money, not losing a lot of money, but getting educated along the way. I learned more about the business and a bit more about how to invest. They weren't winners or losers; there was just some money tied up in a company for a time.

还有相当数量的公司，我买了几股，持有一段时间，然后卖掉——没有赚很多钱，也没有亏很多钱，但在此过程中受到了教育。我对业务有了更多了解，对如何投资也有了更多了解。它们不是赢家也不是输家；只是一些钱暂时被捆绑在一家公司里。

The good news of course is that I got plenty enough right, with enough winners for one investing career to make a financial difference to the lives of my investors and my family.

当然，好消息是我做对了足够多，有足够的成功者，足以让我的投资生涯为我的投资者和我的家庭带来财务上的改变。

**MY YEAR-END PORTFOLIO**

**我的年终投资组合**

As you can see from the results over the last year the value of the portfolio and its evolution have little or indeed nothing to do with my health. Thankfully.

从过去一年的结果可以看出，投资组合的价值及其演变与我的健康几乎没有关系。谢天谢地。

That has been by design for a long time.

长期以来，这都是经过设计的。

A decade ago, I realized I needed to be as comfortable with my investments when I was away from my desk as when I was at it. I was on a long-distance flight on an investment-research trip, and I realized that I thought very differently about Heineken (then in the portfolio) and Nestlé than I did about other companies, where the outcome was more uncertain.

十年前，我意识到，无论我身在何处，我都应该像坐在办公桌前一样，对我的投资感到安心。当时我正在一次投资研究旅行的长途航班上，我发现我对喜力（当时在投资组合中）和雀巢的看法，与我对其他结果更不确定的公司的看法截然不同。

I began shifting away from "binary" outcomes---where the range of results is wide and uncertain---and toward durable or time-friendly compounders, where time is our friend and the range of outcomes runs from "decent" to "superb" with a very low chance of permanent loss.

我开始从“二元”结果——即结果范围广泛且不确定——转向持久或对时间友好的复合增长者，在这些投资中，时间是我们的朋友，结果范围从“不错”到“极好”，且永久性损失的可能性非常低。

That is where the portfolio is already positioned and many of the names are inevitable (as Warren Buffett might call them) and familiar to you---Berkshire Hathaway, Mastercard, American Express, Nestlé, Moody's, and others.

这正是投资组合目前的定位，其中许多名字都是不可避免的（正如沃伦·巴菲特可能称呼的那样），并且你可能很熟悉——Berkshire Hathaway, Mastercard, American Express, Nestlé, Moody's 等。

You can also expect me, over time, to exit investments where there is more of a binary outcome. Companies where outcomes are less certain, like India Energy Exchange, will likely be de-emphasized or removed.

随着时间的推移，你也可以期待我退出那些结果更具二元性的投资。那些结果不那么确定的公司，例如 India Energy Exchange，可能会被淡化或移除。

By contrast, expect me to concentrate on companies where they can rinse, repeat, and grind through. Berkshire Hathaway, for example, now sits on \$350 billion in cash, quietly doing incremental acquisitions---most recently, Occidental's petrochemicals business. As with other "glacial moves" (like swapping Procter & Gamble shares for Duracell), these are subtle but accretive deals, the benefits accruing steadily to Berkshire. Even the much-questioned Kraft Heinz will, in time, likely accrue value to Berkshire.

相比之下，期待我将精力集中在那些能够反复操作并持续努力的公司上。例如，Berkshire Hathaway 目前持有3500亿美元现金，悄悄地进行着增量收购——最近的一次是 Occidental 的石化业务。与其他“缓慢的举动”（比如用 Procter & Gamble 股票换取 Duracell）一样，这些都是微妙但具有增值效应的交易，其利益稳步归属于 Berkshire。即使是备受质疑的 Kraft Heinz，假以时日，也可能为 Berkshire 积累价值。

Similarly with BYD, the thesis is simple. It has the most integrated operations and lowest costs. Yes, there is currently a price war but that means that BYD can drive much-needed consolidation in China and perhaps beyond. Moreover, with its low-cost structure, BYD can choose to return the industry to profitability when it wants.

同样，BYD 的论点也很简单。它拥有最集成的运营和最低的成本。是的，目前存在价格战，但这意味著 BYD 可以在中国乃至更广阔的范围内推动急需的整合。此外，凭借其低成本结构，BYD 可以在需要时选择让行业恢复盈利。

We might own some and study others but whether they're credit rating agencies, luxury names like Ferrari and LVMH, or categories I've studied for years, such as elevators and the unglamorous, cash-generating carpeting and flooring industry, the thread is the same: Wait long enough and you win---maybe a lot, maybe a little, but you win.

我们可能会持有一些，并研究另一些，但无论是信用评级机构，还是 Ferrari 和 LVMH 等奢侈品牌，抑或是我已经研究多年的品类，比如电梯，以及那些不那么光鲜却能创造现金的地毯和地板行业，其主线都是一样的：等待足够长的时间，你就会赢——也许赢得很多，也许赢得一点，但你终究会赢。

The blessing, if there is any, in being forced to devote more of my time to health, is that this long-term, low-maintenance investment approach fits perfectly. I am well prepared for this moment, and confident in telling my family and like-minded investors that, provided you accept my health may from time to time require focus, you are riding alongside me in a strategy built for durable, compounding outcomes.

如果说被迫将更多时间投入健康有什么好处的话，那就是这种长期、低维护的投资方法非常契合。我已经为这一刻做好了充分准备，并充满信心地告诉我的家人和志同道合的投资者，只要你们接受我的健康状况可能不时需要关注，你们就正在与我一同遵循一项旨在实现持久、复利成果的策略。

This shift may even improve return; when I observe Chris Hohn's very focused portfolio, for example, his returns have exceeded mine.

这种转变甚至可能会提高回报；例如，当我观察 Chris Hohn 高度集中的投资组合时，他的回报已经超过了我。

**FUND ORGANIZATION GOING FORWARDS**

**基金组织展望**

We have a great team in place, consisting of Chantal, David, Mariana, and Paaras. They have committed to the transition, or should I say evolution, to a family office.

我们拥有一支优秀的团队，由 Chantal, David, Mariana 和 Paaras 组成。他们已经致力于向家族办公室的转型，或者我应该说，进化。

One of the advantages of the zero management-fee structure where the related shareholders pay an increased management fee is that we do not have to restructure our expenses at Aquamarine and things will continue to run as they have.

零管理费结构的一个优势是，相关股东支付了增加的管理费，这意味着我们无需在 Aquamarine 重组我们的开支，一切将照常运作。

Moreover, my plan is to continue with my investing activities. VALUEx in particular will be a place where I will meet managers and perhaps invest with them---as other invitees have. I will also learn about new investment ideas and potentially partner up with other attendees of VALUEx in exploiting special situations with a small part of the portfolio.

此外，我的计划是继续我的投资活动。VALUEx 将是一个特别的地方，我将在那里会见管理者，并可能与他们一起投资——就像其他受邀者一样。我还将学习新的投资理念，并可能与 VALUEx 的其他参与者合作，利用投资组合中的一小部分资金来发掘特殊情况。

I continue to hold you, my former investors, in high regard. Consider yourselves as members of my extended family. We will do our best to welcome you at VALUEx and we will try to help with other invitations and access as we can.

我将继续高度重视你们，我以前的投资者们。请将自己视为我大家庭的一员。我们将尽力在 VALUEx 欢迎你们，并会尽力协助你们获得其他邀请和机会。

**BEING OPEN ABOUT MY GBM**

**公开我的 GBM 病情**

In this letter I have been explicit about what I've got. I'm doing that deliberately. Although I'm not a public figure like Warren Buffett, or a very famous figure, I'm not unfamous either. People know who I am and I believe there is a strategic reason to be open.

在这封信中，我已经明确说明了我的情况。我这样做是故意的。虽然我不是 Warren Buffett 那样的公众人物，也不是一个非常有名的人物，但我也不算默默无闻。人们知道我是谁，我相信公开是有战略意义的。

If you have a disease that is common enough---or one that allows people to live for a long time---a large interest group naturally forms around it. In the case of glioblastoma (GBM) we face a two-pronged problem. On the one hand it counts as a rare disease, with an incidence of 3--4 per 100,000 of population in developed countries.

如果你患有一种足够常见——或者能让人长期生存——的疾病，那么一个庞大的利益群体就会自然而然地围绕它形成。就胶质母细胞瘤（GBM）而言，我们面临一个两难问题。一方面，它被视为一种罕见病，在发达国家的发病率为每10万人中3-4例。

At the same time, the life expectancy remains short. While there have been many advances in oncology over the last 20 years, not much progress has been made with brain cancer. Most of the anti-cancer therapies don't work in the brain because of the blood-brain barrier. Like the placenta, which limits what enters the fetus, there are many larger molecules that the blood-brain barrier does not let into the brain---which is one reason why GBM remains so difficult to treat.

与此同时，预期寿命仍然很短。尽管过去20年在肿瘤学方面取得了许多进展，但在脑癌方面进展甚微。大多数抗癌疗法在大脑中不起作用，因为存在血脑屏障。就像胎盘限制物质进入胎儿一样，血脑屏障也不允许许多较大的分子进入大脑——这也是 GBM 如此难以治疗的原因之一。

So, we face a "bathtub" problem. If a disease is particularly rare or has a short lifespan--- and both are true for GBM---there isn't much "water" going into the tub, and there is a big drain on the other side. This means that at any one time, there isn't a large focus group to drive solutions.

所以，我们面临一个“浴缸”问题。如果一种疾病特别罕见或寿命短——胶质母细胞瘤（GBM）两者皆是——那么进入浴缸的“水”就不多，而另一边却有一个很大的排水口。这意味着在任何时候，都没有一个庞大的焦点群体来推动解决方案。

Yet for a disease that is statistically rare, GBM has touched my own world with startling frequency. About 10 years ago, while standing in line to enter the TED theatre in Long Beach, I struck up a conversation with the artist Janet Echelman. We discovered that her husband, David, shared my German-Jewish heritage and had been at Harvard Business School at the same time as me. That unexpected connection led to a friendship, and Lory and I were grateful to host them in Zürich and Klosters before David's death from GBM.

然而，对于一种统计学上罕见的疾病，GBM却以惊人的频率触及我的世界。大约十年前，在长滩的TED剧院排队入场时，我与艺术家珍妮特·埃克尔曼聊了起来。我们发现她的丈夫大卫和我一样拥有德国犹太血统，并且和我同时在哈佛商学院学习。这份意想不到的联系促成了一段友谊，我和洛丽曾有幸在苏黎世和克洛斯特斯款待他们，直到大卫因GBM去世。

I encountered it again through the Berkshire Hathaway ecosystem. After Berkshire's 2006 acquisition of Iscar, I traveled to Israel with Whitney Tilson to visit the company and I became friends with Eitan Wertheimer, the son of founder Stef Wertheimer. Eitan, who served as CEO after his father and before handing the reins to Jacob Harpaz, also died of brain cancer.

我通过伯克希尔哈撒韦的生态系统再次遇到了它。在伯克希尔于2006年收购伊斯卡后，我与惠特尼·蒂尔森一同前往以色列参观了该公司，并与创始人斯特夫·韦特海默的儿子伊坦·韦特海默成为了朋友。伊坦在父亲之后担任首席执行官，并将缰绳交给雅各布·哈帕兹之前，也因脑癌去世。

Even within the VALUEx community, the disease has struck close to home: Vitaliy Katsenelson's mother, Irene, and Robert Leitz's father, Siegfried, both succumbed to GBM.

即使在VALUEx社区内，这种疾病也近距离地袭来：维塔利·卡森尼尔森的母亲艾琳，以及罗伯特·莱茨的父亲齐格弗里德，都死于GBM。

I am writing about this because I want to change that empty-bathtub dynamic. It is possible that through my networks, this letter might lead to something that either cures me or prolongs my life. At the very least, it may simply direct attention toward a cure that benefits others, even if it does not happen in my lifetime.

我之所以写这些，是因为我想改变那种空浴缸的动态。通过我的关系网，这封信有可能促成治愈我或延长我生命的事情。至少，它可能会引起人们对治愈方法（即使在我有生之年未能实现）的关注，从而造福他人。

I am aware that it is hard for anyone to know all the research that is out there. With the advent of AI and other new tools, a solution to this might not come from existing cancer research but from other fields entirely.

我清楚任何人都不可能了解所有现有的研究。随着人工智能和其他新工具的出现，解决这个问题的方法可能并非来自现有的癌症研究，而是完全来自其他领域。

So, if anything in this letter prompts you to want to connect me with new therapies or research, please do get in touch. However, I must ask you to follow a very specific protocol: Do not send these ideas to me. Instead, please reach out to my wife, Lory, or to Chantal.

因此，如果这封信中的任何内容促使您希望将我与新疗法或研究联系起来，请务必与我联系。但是，我必须请您遵循一个非常具体的协议：请不要将这些想法发送给我。相反，请联系我的妻子洛丽或尚塔尔。

I have received many expressions of concern, and I truly appreciate them, but here is the reality: Every expression of concern reminds me of what I have, rather than allowing me to live my life. The same is true of miracle cures.

我收到了许多关心的表达，我对此深表感谢，但现实是：每一次关心的表达都提醒我所拥有的，而不是让我过自己的生活。奇迹般的疗法也是如此。

Every other day, I receive a message about a keto diet, stem cell therapy, or a new immunotherapy. I know these are sent with the best of intentions. Nobody intends to cause upset but for me, it means being reminded constantly---every single day---that I have a disease and that there may be "something else" I should be doing. Sometimes these messages come with a certain insistence: "Have you tried this yet?"

每隔一天，我就会收到关于生酮饮食、干细胞疗法或新型免疫疗法的消息。我知道这些都是出于好意。没有人打算引起不安，但对我来说，这意味着每天不断地被提醒我患有疾病，而且可能还有“其他事情”我应该做。有时这些信息带着某种坚持：“你试过这个了吗？”

While I appreciate you thinking of me, this can actually do damage. Instead of being able to take a break from my diagnosis and engage with a friend about an investment idea, due diligence, or a holiday, I am pulled back into the role of a patient. Sometimes, I just want to be your friend again.

虽然我很感谢您对我的关心，但这实际上会造成伤害。我无法从我的诊断中抽身，与朋友讨论投资理念、尽职调查或度假，而是被拉回病人的角色。有时，我只是想再次成为您的朋友。

Please know that the research and ideas you send to Chantal or Lory will not be ignored. I have an excellent medical team at Hirslanden clinic, which is affiliated with the University of Zürich right down the road, with connections to the leading cancer centers in the world.

请您知道，您发送给尚塔尔或洛丽的研究和想法不会被忽视。我在Hirslanden诊所有一支优秀的医疗团队，该诊所隶属于就在附近苏黎世大学，并与世界领先的癌症中心有联系。

**MY MEDICAL TEAM**

**我的医疗团队**

I am fortunate to have a world-class medical team helping me. At Hirslanden, I am profoundly grateful to Professor Nikolai J. Hopf, who has now peered into my functioning brain three times. There is a unique, sacred trust that forms when you allow a surgeon to do that, never knowing exactly what functionality will remain when you wake up. Alongside him, Professor Uwe Schlegel has been invaluable in guiding me through the complexities of neurology and neuro-oncology, as was Professor Nils Peters in my original diagnosis. My radiotherapy and daily oncological care have been expertly managed by Dr. Cristina Picardi and Dr. Jonas Klöpper at Onkozentrum Zürich.

我很幸运拥有一支世界级的医疗团队帮助我。在Hirslanden，我非常感谢尼古拉·J·霍普夫教授，他已经三次审视了我正常运作的大脑。当你允许一位外科医生这样做时，就会形成一种独特而神圣的信任，你永远不知道醒来时还会保留哪些功能。在他身边，乌韦·施莱格尔教授在引导我应对神经病学和神经肿瘤学的复杂性方面发挥了宝贵作用，尼尔斯·彼得斯教授在我的初步诊断中也是如此。我的放射治疗和日常肿瘤护理都由苏黎世肿瘤中心的克里斯蒂娜·皮卡迪医生和乔纳斯·克勒珀医生专业管理。

I have also been able to consult with some of the finest minds and leading researchers in the field. They include Professor Karl Schaller and Professor Denis Migliorini at Geneva University Hospitals (HUG), who provided crucial guidance. Professor Felix Sahm at University Hospital Heidelberg was very kind and took time to talk through his findings with me. In New York, Professor Theodore Schwartz at Weill Cornell offered his expertise. At the University Hospital in Münster, Professor Walter Stummer, Associate Professor Michael Müther, and Dr. Niklas Benedikt Pepper have all contributed their knowledge.

我还曾咨询过该领域一些最杰出的专家和领先研究人员。其中包括日内瓦大学医院 (HUG) 的卡尔·沙勒教授和丹尼斯·米格里奥里尼教授，他们提供了至关重要的指导。海德堡大学医院的费利克斯·萨姆教授非常友善，花时间与我讨论了他的发现。在纽约，威尔康奈尔的西奥多·施瓦茨教授提供了他的专业知识。在明斯特大学医院，沃尔特·斯图默教授、迈克尔·穆瑟副教授和尼克拉斯·贝内迪克特·佩珀医生都贡献了他们的知识。

I must also mention Professor Roger Stupp in Chicago and at the University Hospital Zurich (USZ). I also owe much to the leadership of Professor Michael Weller, as well as Dr. Tobias Weiss, who leads the CSF laboratory and has been at the forefront of the research into my specific case.

我还必须提及芝加哥和苏黎世大学医院（USZ）的罗杰·斯图普教授。我还要感谢迈克尔·韦勒教授的领导，以及托比亚斯·魏斯医生，他领导着脑脊液实验室，并一直走在针对我具体病例研究的最前沿。

These doctors and their teams review every research paper, diet recommendation, and therapy idea that Chantal or Lory forward to them. They and their students read them to see if they apply to my case or to their other patients. There is a lot of data you don't see---detailed brain scans, drug dose adjustments based on swelling, and other variables. To receive a miracle cure from the internet without that context is not helpful.

这些医生和他们的团队会审查尚塔尔或洛丽转发给他们的每一篇研究论文、饮食建议和治疗方案。他们和他们的学生会阅读这些资料，看它们是否适用于我的病例或其他患者。有很多您看不到的数据——详细的脑部扫描、根据肿胀情况调整药物剂量以及其他变量。在没有这些背景的情况下从互联网上获得奇迹般的治疗是无益的。

**THE POWER OF FRIENDSHIP**

**友谊的力量**

Douglas Murray has written, in the context of the October 7th massacres, that sometimes a flare goes up, and you see *exactly* where everyone is standing. My cancer diagnosis was one of those flares. When things fall apart, you discover who your friends really are---a crisis has a way of illuminating what was always there.

道格拉斯·默里在谈及10月7日大屠杀时写道，有时一颗信号弹升空，你就能“清楚地”看到每个人所处的位置。我的癌症诊断就是这样一颗信号弹。当事情分崩离析时，你才会发现谁才是你真正的朋友——危机总能揭示那些一直存在的东西。

I have been blessed with many who showed up without hesitation. Not everyone knew how to, and that is understandable---but what has stayed with me are the people who turned out to have extraordinary reserves of kindness and presence.

我很幸运有很多人毫不犹豫地伸出援手。不是每个人都知道该怎么做，这可以理解——但让我铭记在心的是那些展现出非凡善良和存在感的人。

Some people instinctively run *into* the fire. Bryan Lawrence and Gillian Segal and Naama Baram did just that. Others dropped everything to travel and visit: Li Lu flew in from Seattle, Mohnish Pabrai from Austin, Bonnie and Arthur Fish from Toronto, Adam Franks from London and Ken Shubin Stein from New York, as did John Mihaljevic, Danny Aegerter and others in Zurich. Their physical presence meant far more than they realized.

有些人本能地冲进火海。布莱恩·劳伦斯、吉莉安·西格尔和娜玛·巴拉姆就是这样做的。还有人放下一切，前来探望：李录从西雅图飞来，莫尼什·帕布莱从奥斯汀飞来，邦妮和阿瑟·菲什从多伦多飞来，亚当·弗兰克斯从伦敦飞来，肯·舒宾·斯坦从纽约飞来，约翰·米哈尔耶维奇、丹尼·艾格尔特和苏黎世的其他朋友也是如此。他们的亲身到场意义远超他们的想象。

The World Minds community showed up in force. Rolf Dobelli was present for one of my focal seizures and didn't flinch. Kipper Blakeley, Simone Schürle, Laura Baudis, and Ashkan Nikeghbali have each shown up when it mattered, demonstrating the depth of connection that network has fostered. Dan Ariely spent an entire day with me after the World Minds annual meeting, offering not just his friendship but the kind of understanding that comes from someone who has experienced life-altering tragedy at a young age.

世界智者社区倾力支持。罗尔夫·多贝利在我一次局灶性癫痫发作时在场，他没有退缩。基珀·布莱克利、西蒙娜·舒勒、劳拉·鲍迪斯和阿什坎·尼克加利都在关键时刻出现，展现了该网络所培养的深厚联系。丹·艾瑞里在世界智者年会后与我共度了一整天，他不仅提供了友谊，还提供了那种只有经历过人生巨变（在年幼时）的人才能理解的理解。

Here in Zürich, our family is a member of the Chabad Jewish community. Mendel Rosenfeld and his son-in-law Nochum have been extraordinarily kind, visiting at short notice. And when Scholom---Mendel's brother---arranged a community Shabbat, he said words that brought me to tears: *"You do what the doctors tell you to do*---*but only the Almighty knows your fate."*

在苏黎世，我们家是哈巴德犹太社区的一员。门德尔·罗森菲尔德和他的女婿诺楚姆对我们非常友善，接到通知后短时间内就来探望。当门德尔的兄弟斯科洛姆安排了一次社区安息日时，他说的话让我泪流满面：“你做医生告诉你的事情——但只有全能者知道你的命运。”

Then there are those who simply read about my diagnosis and reached out with a kind word. Atul Gawande, who wrote about me in *The Checklist Manifesto*, reached out after reading my earlier letter. I had not known he followed my annual updates. So did Nicholas Christakis and Amar Bhide. All offered medical insight, humanity, and perspective at a moment when I needed all three. **HOW TO TALK ABOUT HEALTH TO SOMEONE WHO IS SICK**

**如何与病人谈论健康**

One more thing: If you want to understand how to talk to someone who's sick, read Letty Pogrebin's, *How to Be a Friend to a Friend Who's Sick.* There is an excellent *Wall Street Journal* review, or I can send you a copy if I have extras.

还有一件事：如果你想了解如何与病人交谈，请阅读莱蒂·波格雷宾的《如何成为生病朋友的朋友》。《华尔街日报》有一篇精彩的评论，如果我有多的副本，我可以寄给你。

Pogrebin writes: "For a sick friend, first do no harm." The basic idea is that a lot of normal conversation is just inappropriate or does damage when you're talking to somebody who's either sick or terminally ill. For example, if one person asks me how I am, that's fine, but if I have 10 people a day asking me how I am, and I need to respond to every single one, that can be very difficult. What can help is to say, "Just thinking of you," without requiring me to respond.

波格雷宾写道：“对于生病的朋友，首先要不造成伤害。”基本思想是，当你与生病或绝症患者交谈时，许多正常的对话都是不恰当的或会造成伤害的。例如，如果一个人问我身体怎么样，那没关系，但如果我每天有10个人问我身体怎么样，而我需要回应每一个人，那可能会非常困难。有所帮助的做法是说：“只是想着你”，而不需要我回应。

If you want to get in touch with me about things that are of common interest, things outside of my medical situation that allow me to live my life to the fullest, then that's great. But if you have medical suggestions, please send them to Lory or to Chantal.

如果你想就我们共同感兴趣的事情，或我的医疗状况之外能让我充实生活的事情与我联系，那太好了。但如果你有医疗方面的建议，请发送给 Lory 或 Chantal。

**HINENI**

In closing there is a Hebrew word I keep coming back to: *Hineni*. It means "Here I am." But it also means so much more.

最后，有一个希伯来词我反复思考：*Hineni*。它的意思是“我在这里”。但它的含义远不止于此。

It appears in the Hebrew Bible at moments of profound testing. When God calls Abraham to sacrifice his son, Abraham responds, "Hineni." And as they walk up the mountain and Isaac calls out, "Father," Abraham answers him with the same word: "Hineni, my son."

它出现在希伯来圣经中，在经历严峻考验的时刻。当上帝呼唤亚伯拉罕献祭他的儿子时，亚伯拉罕回应道：“Hineni。”当他们走上山，以撒喊道：“父亲！”亚伯拉罕用同样的词回答他：“Hineni，我的儿子。”

It also appears when Moses hears the call of God before the burning bush, and when Isaiah volunteers for his mission. In every instance, Hineni signifies that the protagonist is ready to do what the Almighty asks---before he even knows what will be asked.

它也出现在摩西在燃烧的荆棘前听到上帝的呼唤时，以及以赛亚主动承担他的使命时。在每一个例子中，Hineni 都表示主人公已准备好执行全能者的要求——甚至在他知道将被要求什么之前。

In a sense, Hineni is the act of letting go of the script you wrote for yourself and accepting the one that is being written for you. I find myself saying Hineni now. Here I am. I don't know what comes next, but I am here to meet it. My task has shifted. For 28 years, my job was to compound your capital. That is no longer my primary task. The task now is to confront what has been given to me---without complaint.

从某种意义上说，Hineni 是一种放下你为自己编写的剧本，接受正在为你编写的剧本的行为。我发现我现在正说着 Hineni。我在这里。我不知道接下来会发生什么，但我已准备好去面对。我的任务已经转变。在过去的28年里，我的工作是为您积累资本。那不再是我的主要任务。现在的任务是面对我所拥有的一切——没有抱怨。

Sometimes the music you prepared to play isn't the music you're called to play. There is a moment that captures this perfectly: The pianist Maria João Pires is seated at the Royal Concertgebouw in Amsterdam. As Riccardo Chailly leads the orchestra into Mozart's Piano Concerto No. 20, you see her jolt---she had prepared a different concerto entirely. There is a split second of visible fear, and then she steadies herself, turns to the keyboard, and plays the correct concerto brilliantly.

有时你准备演奏的音乐并不是你被要求演奏的音乐。有一个瞬间完美地捕捉了这一点：钢琴家玛丽亚·若昂·皮雷斯坐在阿姆斯特丹皇家音乐厅。当里卡多·夏伊指挥乐团演奏莫扎特第20号钢琴协奏曲时，你看到她猛地一惊——她完全准备了另一首协奏曲。有一瞬间明显的恐惧，然后她镇定下来，转向键盘，出色地演奏了正确的协奏曲。

That is where I am. I was prepared to play the music of compounding capital for decades to come, but that is not the music I have been given. And---unlike Maria Pires at the Concertgebouw---there is no score, so I will have to improvise, to the best of my ability.

这就是我所处的位置。我曾准备在未来几十年里演奏复利资本的乐章，但那不是我被赋予的音乐。而且——不像皇家音乐厅的玛丽亚·皮雷斯——没有乐谱，所以我必须尽我所能地即兴创作。

*Hineni.* Here I am.

*Hineni.* 我在这里。
